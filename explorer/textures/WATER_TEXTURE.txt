Vista uses this linear-data normal map for subtle surface ripples in Context
only, with an illustrative turquoise colour wash inspired by the user's
reference. Tile scales are artistic choices, not measured waves. Water is
opaque; colour does not represent depth, surveyed bathymetry or water quality.
The mapped coastline and water mesh are unchanged. A procedural fallback is
used if the optional image is unavailable. Plain and Night retain their styles.
