        Where this comes from

        Table 1 of the Public Views Guidelines (pp.4–6) lists 24 views. Plan cones: City open-data view-cones polygons, mapped with a transformation checked against 8 OpenStreetMap footprints (max 0.1 m). Origins are the polygon apexes; the by-laws are authoritative. Cone heights are not published in the dataset and are never invented. The wedge is exploratory camera geometry, not the City cone. Mountains: NASA SRTM via Open Topo Data, approximate, observer view only.

        Technical details

        Model readout

        Data status

        Heights: OSM, unverified Terrain z: model-relative View cones: plan only, heights unknown Extension: OSM + SRTM, all origins covered Mountains: SRTM backdrop, approximate Synthetic checker ↗
        • Context: source Rhino geometry is preserved. When rebuilt context is available, ground and water are replaced and building bases are aligned to it; XY footprints and building heights are retained.
        • View cones and origins: City open-data polygons converted with an equirectangular mapping about the earth anchor, identified from and checked against 8 OpenStreetMap footprints (max residual 0.1 m). Absolute accuracy inherits OpenStreetMap's.
        • Heights: the model's z datum is unknown; cone heights are not in the open data; the mountain backdrop is SRTM-derived and approximate. Every check is a declared hypothesis.
        • Clauses 3.1.2–3.1.5 describe exceptions that people decide; the tool reports numbers and flags them.
